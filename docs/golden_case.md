# Case Demonstration: China Molecular Diagnostics

## 1. Purpose

This case is the highest-confidence demonstration and evaluation baseline for Industry Analyst OS. It does not restrict the application to healthcare or molecular diagnostics.

## 2. Industry

**Primary market:** China molecular diagnostics  
**Current-state window:** 2024–2026  
**Foresight horizon:** 2026–2030  
**Geography:** China, with international benchmarks where relevant

Initial areas to examine:

- conventional and real-time PCR;
- digital PCR;
- NGS-related molecular diagnostics;
- molecular point-of-care testing;
- instruments and reagents;
- selected testing-service models;
- infectious disease, oncology, reproductive health, genetic disease, and companion diagnostics.

The final inclusion and exclusion boundary must be generated and confirmed during Research Setup rather than assumed here.

## 3. Fictional company

**GeneMatrix Diagnostics｜基矩诊断**

Initial profile:

- Chinese domestic IVD company;
- existing PCR instruments and reagent portfolio;
- hospital customers and distributor network;
- established presence in infectious-disease testing;
- considering expansion into oncology, digital PCR, and NGS;
- limited capital and R&D resources;
- cannot pursue every technical direction simultaneously;
- limited bioinformatics capability;
- limited national oncology expert network;
- faces both domestic and multinational competitors.

These assumptions will later become editable company inputs, not hard-coded facts.

## 4. Decision question

How should GeneMatrix Diagnostics allocate limited resources between 2026 and 2030?

Strategic options to compare:

1. strengthen the existing PCR franchise;
2. enter digital PCR;
3. build an internal NGS platform;
4. partner with an NGS platform provider;
5. enter oncology companion diagnostics;
6. develop an integrated molecular-diagnostics solution;
7. invest in or acquire a complementary company;
8. defer selected investments while monitoring trigger conditions.

## 5. Required outputs

- defensible market definition;
- industry and value-chain map;
- competitor landscape and company comparison;
- market drivers and constraints;
- future competition, business-model, and customer-demand scenarios;
- GeneMatrix market fit, advantages, gaps, risks, and readiness;
- ranked strategic options;
- 90-day, 12-month, and three-year Action Plan;
- evidence matrix, counter-evidence, confidence, and human approvals.

## 6. Accuracy expectation

The molecular-diagnostics Industry Pack will contain more specialized terminology, dimensions, methods, and evaluation cases than general research mode. Therefore:

- this case should produce the most complete and professionally reviewable output;
- other industries must still complete the same universal workflow;
- unsupported domain-specific conclusions in other industries must be marked as lower-confidence or require a new Industry Pack/expert input;
- no molecular-diagnostics assumptions may leak into unrelated industries.
